## Key Takeaways

- **The Pragmatic Engineer** is the largest newsletter (1M+ subs, 2x/week)
- **InfoQ** is the most comprehensive single source (blog, podcast, newsletter, YouTube)
- **ByteByteGo** and **Fireship** are highest-reach YouTube for system design
- **Architecture Weekly** by Oskar Dudycz is the top dedicated architecture newsletter
- **Martin Fowler's blog** and **Thoughtworks Technology Radar** remain foundational references
- **Software Engineering Radio** is the longest-running relevant podcast (since 2006)
