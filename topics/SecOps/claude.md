## Key Takeaways

- **For daily news**: The Hacker News, Dark Reading, and SecurityWeek are highest-volume
- **For curated digests**: tl;dr sec and Risky Bulletin are the two most respected newsletters
- **For podcasts**: Darknet Diaries (storytelling), Risky Business (news/analysis)
- **For YouTube**: John Hammond and SANS Institute for SecOps/SOC work
- **For community**: Snyk DevSecCon Discord and OWASP Slack are the most active dedicated communities
