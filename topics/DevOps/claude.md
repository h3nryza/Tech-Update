## Key Takeaways

- **For daily news**: DevOps.com, The New Stack, and InfoQ provide the most comprehensive coverage
- **For structured learning**: TechWorld with Nana and KodeKloud on YouTube
- **For weekly digests**: DevOps Weekly and KubeWeekly are the gold-standard newsletters
- **For real-time community**: Reddit r/devops and CNCF Slack are the most active
- **For audio**: Ship It! and The Cloudcast are the most consistently published podcasts
