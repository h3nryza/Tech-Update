# DevOps

| Type | Source | Link | Why it matters | Est. releases / week | Popularity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Newsletter | DevOps Weekly | https://www.devopsweekly.com/ | Still one of the best concise weekly digests for tools, platform shifts, and community writing. | 1 | Very High | Best baseline monitoring feed. |
| News site | The New Stack | https://thenewstack.io/ | Best broad practitioner news site for cloud native, platform, DevOps, and infra tooling. | 10-20 | Very High | Strong editorial filter for what actually matters. |
| Podcast | DevOps Paradox | https://www.devopsparadox.com/ | Long-running, practical podcast with strong cultural and operational coverage. | 1 | High | Good for trend interpretation. |

## Editorial Use

- Prioritize the official release feed and official blog for factual launch details.
- Use the video/channel source for demos, screenshots, and product-tour context.
- Use the podcast/community source for interpretation, sentiment, and downstream adoption signals.
