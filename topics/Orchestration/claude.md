## Key Takeaways

- **Kubernetes Official Blog** and **CNCF Blog** are the two most authoritative sources
- **KubeWeekly** (curated by CNCF) is the must-subscribe weekly newsletter
- **LWKD** is essential for tracking K8s development (PRs/merges)
- **Kubernetes Slack** (170+ channels at slack.k8s.io) is the largest real-time community
- **KubeCon** events (3 per year) are the premier gatherings for the cloud-native ecosystem
