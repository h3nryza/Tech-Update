## Key Takeaways

- **SRE Weekly** is the single most-cited dedicated SRE resource (weekly since 2015)
- **Google SRE** (sre.google) remains authoritative -- free SRE Book, Workbook, and Prodcast
- **DevOps, SRE & Infrastructure Discord** (22K+ members) is the largest real-time chat community
- **awesome-sre on GitHub** is the best curated link collection for SRE resources
- SRE-specific podcasts publish less frequently than broader DevOps podcasts
