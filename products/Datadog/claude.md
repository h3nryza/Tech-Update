## Key Takeaways

- **The Monitor** blog is the single most important source (product updates, engineering deep-dives, "State of" reports)
- **"This Month in Datadog"** is a great monthly roundup (blog + video)
- **Datadog Public Slack** (chat.datadoghq.com) is the most active community
- **DASH 2026**: June 9-10, North Javits Center, NYC -- biggest annual Datadog event
