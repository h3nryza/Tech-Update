## Key Takeaways

- **For product updates**: Slack What's New + Slack Blog News section are primary
- **For developer updates**: Slack Developer Changelog is essential for API/platform changes
- **Since Salesforce acquired Slack**: Salesforce Ben and Salesforce Newsroom are critical for major announcements
- **For broader tech coverage**: TechCrunch's Slack tag page has most frequent coverage
- **No single podcast is purely Slack-focused** anymore; Customer Success Podcast is the closest
