# Slack

Best sources for Slack feature drops, workflow/AI updates, platform changes, and video coverage. Research date: 2026-03-12.

| Type | Source | Link | Why it matters | Est. releases / week | Popularity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Official release feed | Slack What's New | https://slack.com/whats-new | Best single page for recent product releases; active with feature-release entries in 2026. | 1-3 | Very High | Current signal: page listed updates from January and February 2026. |
| Official articles | Slack Feature Drops | https://slack.com/blog/news/feature-drop | Best source for packaged monthly-ish product storytelling and screenshots. | 0.25-1 | High | Current signal: January 2026 feature drop was indexed. |
| Official support changelog | Slack updates and changes | https://slack.com/help/articles/115004846068-Slack-updates-and-changes. | Practical source for shipped behavior changes, admin-impacting updates, and smaller product tweaks. | 2-5 | High | Useful when the blog is too selective. |
| Video / channel | Slack YouTube | https://www.youtube.com/@Slack | Best official video source for product demos, events, and AI/workflow explainers. | 1-4 | High | Volume is lower than cloud platforms, but quality is good. |
| Platform / developer | Slack Platform Blog | https://slack.dev/blog/ | Best source if your audience cares about app platform, automations, and agent integrations. | 0.5-2 | Medium | Important for builders rather than end users. |

## Editorial Use

- Prioritize the official release feed and official blog for factual launch details.
- Use the video/channel source for demos, screenshots, and product-tour context.
- Use the podcast/community source for interpretation, sentiment, and downstream adoption signals.
