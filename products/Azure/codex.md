# Azure

Best sources for Azure platform changes, service launches, weekly video recaps, and partner-facing updates. Research date: 2026-03-12.

| Type | Source | Link | Why it matters | Est. releases / week | Popularity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Official releases | Azure Updates | https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/updates/ | Canonical Azure release feed; best first stop for new services, previews, GA, and retirements. | 10-25 | Very High | High shipping frequency across infra, data, AI, and security. |
| Official articles | Microsoft Community Hub: Azure | https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/category/azure/blog/azure | Good for rollout details, migration notes, and broader announcements not obvious in the raw update feed. | 3-8 | High | Partner and product team posts are both worth watching. |
| Video / channel | Microsoft Azure YouTube | https://www.youtube.com/@MicrosoftAzure | Best official channel for demos, event sessions, Azure Friday clips, and product explainers. | 5-15 | Very High | Strong source during Build, Ignite, and large AI launches. |
| Community video | John Savill's Technical Training | https://www.youtube.com/@NTFAQGuy | Best recurring Azure weekly recap. Search results show weekly updates were still active in March 2026. | 1-4 | Very High | High signal for practitioners; especially useful for Azure infra and ops. |
| Podcast | The Azure Podcast | https://azpodcast.azurewebsites.net/ | Long-running community podcast that tracks Azure releases and interviews product teams. | 1 | Medium | Best audio source if you want Azure-specific commentary. |

## Editorial Use

- Prioritize the official release feed and official blog for factual launch details.
- Use the video/channel source for demos, screenshots, and product-tour context.
- Use the podcast/community source for interpretation, sentiment, and downstream adoption signals.
