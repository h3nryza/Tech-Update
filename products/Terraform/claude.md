## Key Takeaways

- **Terraform Weekly** by Anton Babenko is the single best curated newsletter in the ecosystem
- **r/Terraform** on Reddit (~70k members) is the most active community hub
- **HashiCorp Discuss** is the official forum with HashiCorp employee participation
- **Day Two DevOps** by Ned Bellavance is the most Terraform-focused podcast
- **Spacelift Blog** publishes the most frequent and in-depth Terraform technical content
- Terraform CLI releases happen ~every 2 weeks; Enterprise moved to quarterly releases (Aug 2025)
