## Innovation Weeks (High-Volume Periods)

Cloudflare runs several Innovation Weeks per year with 10-20+ posts/week:
- **Birthday Week** (late September)
- **Security Week**
- **Developer Week**
- **Speed Week**

Tracked at: https://www.cloudflare.com/innovation-week/

## Key Takeaways

- **The Cloudflare Blog** is the single most important source -- deeply technical posts, product news, and security research
- **Cloudflare Developers Discord** (86k+ members) is the most active real-time community
- **This Week in NET** on Cloudflare TV is the best weekly recap show
- During Innovation Weeks, blog output surges to 10-20+ posts/week
