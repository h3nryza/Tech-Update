# AWS

Best current sources for AWS announcements, launches, deep dives, videos, and weekly audio coverage. Research date: 2026-03-12.

| Type | Source | Link | Why it matters | Est. releases / week | Popularity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Official releases | AWS News Blog | https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/ | Primary launch feed; AWS was still posting weekly roundup entries in February 2026. | 5-10 | Very High | Latest signal seen: 2026-03-09; weekly roundup cadence is active. |
| Official release feed | AWS What's New | https://aws.amazon.com/new/ | Broadest release feed across services; best source for day-to-day shipping volume. | 20-40 | Very High | Use RSS or filtering by service. |
| Podcast | Official AWS Podcast | https://aws.amazon.com/podcasts/aws-podcast/ | Best AWS-owned long-form audio source for launches, re:Invent fallout, and practitioner interviews. | 1-2 | High | Good companion to the two official written feeds. |
| Video / channel | AWS YouTube | https://www.youtube.com/@amazonwebservices | Fastest place to catch re:Invent sessions, product demos, Black Belt talks, and launch explainers. | 15-40 | Very High | High volume around events; searchable playlists are strong. |
| Community news | Last Week in AWS | https://www.lastweekinaws.com/ | Best independent AWS watcher for opinionated filtering, newsletter coverage, and a podcast/news brief. | 3-7 | High | Useful when official feeds are too noisy. |

## Editorial Use

- Prioritize the official release feed and official blog for factual launch details.
- Use the video/channel source for demos, screenshots, and product-tour context.
- Use the podcast/community source for interpretation, sentiment, and downstream adoption signals.
