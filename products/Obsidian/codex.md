# Obsidian

Best sources for Obsidian app releases, community plugin movement, videos, and recurring community curation. Research date: 2026-03-12.

| Type | Source | Link | Why it matters | Est. releases / week | Popularity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Official articles | Obsidian Blog | https://obsidian.md/blog/ | Best official source for core-app releases, roadmap-adjacent posts, and major plugin/tooling announcements. | 0.1-0.5 | Very High | Official release cadence is lower, but every post matters. |
| Community release feed | Obsidian Roundup | https://www.obsidianroundup.org/ | Best weekly digest for plugins, themes, workflows, and ecosystem movement. | 1 | Very High | Highest-signal community monitoring source. |
| Official forum | Obsidian Forum: Announcements | https://forum.obsidian.md/c/announcements/13 | Best place to catch release posts, plugin announcements, and maintainer notes quickly. | 3-10 | High | Good for both official and community changes. |
| Video / channel | Obsidian YouTube | https://www.youtube.com/@obsidianmd | Best official video source for demos, community talks, and feature walkthroughs. | 0.25-1 | Medium | Use community channels to fill the gap between official videos. |
| Community video | Nicole van der Hoeven YouTube | https://www.youtube.com/@nicolevdh | One of the most consistently useful community channels for Obsidian workflows and release coverage. | 1-3 | High | Strong for turning updates into actionable content. |

## Editorial Use

- Prioritize the official release feed and official blog for factual launch details.
- Use the video/channel source for demos, screenshots, and product-tour context.
- Use the podcast/community source for interpretation, sentiment, and downstream adoption signals.
